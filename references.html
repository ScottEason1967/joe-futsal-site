Coach profile from the FutsalUOS IPDP

Verbatim from the "Current Player Profile" section of Joe's Individual Player Development Plan, authored by his head coach at FutsalUOS.

Coach Profile · IPDP

Joe is an athletic, physically strong, technically excellent pivot who can protect the ball, beat players 1v1 and finish. His current focus is becoming a more effective focal point in possession: securing the ball under pressure, choosing the right moment to retain or release it, and linking more consistently with wing players. The aim is to make his movement and touches purposeful so he brings others into the attack and creates better options around him.

— Tony Loftus · Head Coach, FutsalUOS · Individual Player Development Plan
