Beyond the pivot
Athlete first

Joe's futsal sits on a serious senior-football base — central midfield and centre-back at Steps 4 and 5 of the English non-league pyramid, on top of nine years of independent strength & conditioning. That athletic platform is exactly what the modern pivot is built on: the frame to hold play up, the power to win duels, and the engine to press and recover.

He is also genuinely quick — verified elite times across 5m, 10m and 30m, topping out at 34 km/h. Rare pace on a 6'2", 89.9kg frame, and exactly what turns a hold-up into a run in behind the last line.
