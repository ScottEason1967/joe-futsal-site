The background

My up-to-date, independently verifiable case as a pivot, measured against England Futsal’s Pride DNA. I was invited to the England U23 assessment day in Derby, then had to withdraw with a high ankle sprain — damage to the lateral ligament (ATFL) and the syndesmosis (AITFL).

I’m now in full rehab, aiming to be fit for the England U23 session at Bedford in mid-October. If I can’t make it in time, this site is the competitive evidence I would have shown there — and a measure of how much I want to be part of the England setup.
